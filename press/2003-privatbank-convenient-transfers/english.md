PrivatBank, the leader of Ukraine’s payment card market, and Visa International, a leading international payment system, announced today the launch in Ukraine of an international pilot project offering a new service called “Convenient Transfers.” The project enables all of the bank’s customers and other residents of Ukraine to transfer money to Visa cards worldwide.

According to PrivatBank Chairman Alexander Dubilet, “The new service gives customers truly unique opportunities to transfer money. A ‘Convenient Transfer’ can be made at any of PrivatBank’s more than 1,400 branches across Ukraine—from a bank account, a payment card, or in cash. The recipient may be the holder of a Visa payment card issued by virtually any Ukrainian or foreign bank.”

Mr Dubilet added: “The fact that Ukraine’s PrivatBank became one of the first banks in the world to offer this service together with Visa International demonstrates both the bank’s commitment to the most advanced technologies and the considerable social value of those technologies. Today, together with Visa, we are presenting more than a new service; we are introducing a new approach to financial relationships between people—P2P, or Person to Person. This new method of transferring money is convenient and more affordable than existing mechanisms.” Mr Dubilet also thanked Visa International for making it possible to implement the project within such a short period.

Brian Hackett, Executive Vice President of Visa CEMEA, said: “I would like to congratulate PrivatBank on the successful implementation of this project, which required considerable effort from the bank. We are honoured that PrivatBank became the first bank in the CIS to introduce this new service. I would also like to note that the bank completed all the necessary preparations quickly and efficiently. The concept of money transfers is not new to the financial services market, but the methods currently available are often slow, expensive, and difficult to use. Responding to requests from its member banks, Visa developed a solution that enables banks to offer a new service: a fast and simple way to transfer money from one Visa card to another. The recipient can be anywhere in the world and does not need to take any action to receive the money. All that is required is a Visa card.”

PrivatBank continued to develop the new service actively, seeking to make it available to as many customers as possible. Alexander Vityaz, Head of PrivatBank’s Electronic Business Centre, reported that “Convenient Transfers” could already be made through the Privat24 internet banking system as well as at bank branches. Holders of Visa payment cards issued by PrivatBank also received additional benefits, including a preferential transfer fee and access to Ukraine’s largest card service network. The system’s capabilities were expected to expand further, allowing card-to-card transfers to be made not only at branches and through Privat24, but also via ATMs, GSM banking, and an email-based online transfer system.

Participants in the PrivatBank and Visa International press conference in Kyiv were able to experience the advantages of the new service firsthand and become some of its first customers. During the press conference, each journalist was given the opportunity to make a transfer at a bank branch to one of their personal Visa cards, and most representatives of the Ukrainian media used the service.

## About Visa International

Visa payment products are used and preferred worldwide. Visa is the world’s largest international payment system. Annual turnover on Visa cards exceeds USD 2.4 trillion. Visa cards are accepted at more than 30 million merchant locations, including 810,000 ATMs worldwide. Visa plays a decisive role in introducing new payment products and technologies for its 21,000 member financial institutions and cardholders. Visa is a leader in internet payments and promotes universal commerce—the ability to conduct business at any time, in any place, and through multiple forms of communication.

## About PrivatBank

PrivatBank is one of Ukraine’s largest banks, with a nationwide service network of 1,415 branches and offices. The bank holds leading positions in the country’s principal banking segments and is the acknowledged national leader in the payment card market. By the date of publication, PrivatBank had issued more than 2.7 million payment cards—42% of all cards issued by Ukrainian banks—including 1.8 million Visa cards. Its card service network comprised 765 ATMs, more than 15,000 merchant and service locations, and 1,873 cash withdrawal points. In 2002, a PrivatBank representative was elected to the Visa CEMEA Regional Board of Directors, and the bank was admitted to Visa CEMEA’s elite “Millionaires Club.” Introducing new banking technologies was a strategic development priority for PrivatBank. More than 25,000 customers were using the Privat24 electronic banking system, while the GSM banking system had over 30,000 users.
