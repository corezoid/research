## PrivatBank Became Ukraine's First Multimillion-Card Issuer

By the date of publication, PrivatBank had issued more than four million payment cards through the international Visa and MasterCard payment systems. PrivatBank Chairman Alexander Dubilet announced the milestone during a press conference at UNIAN. According to him, developing the most advanced technologies and introducing new, forward-looking products had always been—and remained—among the bank’s principal priorities. Since the beginning of 2003, the number of cards issued by the bank had increased 1.8-fold, while its market share had reached 43%.

Increasing card issuance volumes, however, was not an end in itself for the bank. PrivatBank’s card-market development programme was comprehensive, encompassing both the expansion of its card service network and the capabilities of its card products. At the time, the bank’s nationwide card service network included approximately 1,200 ATMs and more than 13,000 POS terminals across Ukraine.

“When more than 300,000 pensioners, hundreds of thousands of students, and employees of industrial enterprises use payment cards, it shows that our technologies are clear, convenient, and valuable to every customer, regardless of income or place of residence,” Alexander Dubilet said.

During 2003, PrivatBank offered its customers several distinctive card products, most notably instantly issued payment cards. PrivatBank also became the first bank in Ukraine to begin issuing Visa payment cards with embedded microchips.

“In the near future, we will offer several more interesting products and services that will significantly change the traditional model of interaction between the bank and its customers,” Alexander Dubilet said.

Bank representatives reported that the four-millionth card was issued to Viktor Pastushenko, a PrivatBank customer from Vinnytsia who had ordered a “Photo Card” in December—a distinctive product featuring its holder’s personalised design. The commemorative card was presented to the customer during the press conference. PrivatBank also gave him a commemorative gift: a certificate for a ten-day trip for two to the Red Sea coast, including accommodation at the five-star InterContinental hotel.

PrivatBank’s management regarded ATM-based registration for the Privat24 internet banking service and the creation of an online Interactive Service Centre as the bank’s most revolutionary payment-card innovations of 2003.

Alexander Vityaz, Head of PrivatBank’s Electronic Business Centre, described ATM registration for Privat24 as the fastest, most accessible, and most convenient way to connect to the internet banking service. Privat24 gave cardholders virtually unrestricted access to their funds without visiting a bank branch, at any time and from anywhere in Ukraine or abroad.

When assistance was required, the bank’s Interactive Service Centre provided a fast and convenient channel of communication with PrivatBank. Customer interaction with the bank was not restricted by time or by the amount of information exchanged. Through the Interactive Service Centre, customers could obtain information online, block a lost card, learn how to use internet banking in real time, and access other services. This distinguished the online service from conventional telephone or email communication with the bank.

The issuance of PrivatBank’s four-millionth payment card was described as a significant event for both the bank and Ukraine’s wider banking community.

“PrivatBank was the first domestic bank to identify the development of modern cashless payment systems as a priority. Today, the bank has a nationwide payment-card service network and millions of customers who prefer cashless payments using a payment card,” said a message to the bank from Serhiy Tihipko, Governor of the National Bank of Ukraine.

The NBU Governor also noted that the experience PrivatBank had accumulated over the years represented an asset for the entire banking system and provided a foundation for developing a national system of cashless consumer payments and a regulatory framework supporting the introduction of modern banking technologies in Ukraine.

PrivatBank was a leader in Ukraine’s payment-card market and one of the country’s banks that substantially influenced technological development trends across the banking sector, according to a message addressed to the bank by Oleksandr Suhoniako, President of the Association of Ukrainian Banks.

“Thanks to your activity, the most advanced technologies and services—those that seemed like science fiction only two or three years ago—have become familiar to millions of customers. We are confident that PrivatBank’s continued work will help strengthen public trust in the banking system and introduce new standards of banking service in Ukraine,” the message said.
