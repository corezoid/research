# Press releases and news items

Historical press releases and contemporaneous news coverage of the products and technologies
later narrated in the [interviews](../interviews/) and formalized in the research corpus. Where
an interview is recollection, a press release is a dated primary record: it fixes what was
launched, when, and in what words it was announced at the time.

Like interviews, these are **not** part of the citation graph — they are context, not
contributions, and links to papers are "see also" only. Originals are linked, never re-hosted;
only English translations prepared for this repository are committed here.
