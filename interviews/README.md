# Interviews and public conversations

Public interviews and profiles documenting the development of ideas later formalized in the
Corezoid Research corpus. They are collected here as primary sources: they show when a concept
first surfaced in public and in what words, years before it was written up as a paper.

Interviews are **not** part of the citation graph in the root [README](../README.md) — they are
context, not contributions. Links to papers below are therefore "see also" only.
